**AI Engineering Capstone — AI Test Failure Investigation Agent**

I have completed and deployed Release 1 of my AI Test Failure Investigation Agent.

The application uses five Google ADK agents to analyze pytest failures, group related errors, investigate possible root causes, retrieve relevant technical documentation and historical cases, and generate evidence-based troubleshooting reports.

The system also includes Human-on-Demand clarification, persistent episodic memory, automated end-to-end tests, and Langfuse observability.
